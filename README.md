## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the bespoke sophistication of high-end grooming in Dakhla, Morocco—where Atlantic winds meet Saharan dunes and classic craftsmanship meets refined modernity. 

The atmosphere evokes exclusivity, masculine poise, and uncompromising attention to craft: dark marble countertops, hand-forged Japanese steel shears, vintage cognac leather chairs, and subtle amber lighting. 

The style combines **Editorial Dark Luxury** and **Subtle Glassmorphism**:
- Deep, obsidian backdrops establish privacy and serenity.
- Brushed gold metallic accents signal precision and heritage.
- Translucent charcoal glass panels with razor-thin gold and charcoal borders provide dimensional depth without visual clutter.
- Bento-grid architecture organizes services, master barbers, appointments, and grooming rituals into deliberate, magazine-grade showcases.

## Colors

The palette is strictly calibrated for dark-mode opulence, balancing deep carbon black surfaces against polished gold accents and warm bone typography.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The hallmark tone of master razor craft. Reserved for key calls to action, badges of prestige, active states, and focal brand moments.
- **Secondary Champagne Gold (`#E5C158`)**: High-luminance accent for highlights, hover transitions, and decorative rules.
- **Tertiary Antique Gold (`#997A15`)**: Deep muted gold for subtle framing, dividers, and low-priority decorative indicators.
- **Obsidian Dark (`#0B0B0C`)**: Canvas background evoking Moroccan night skies and polished stone.

### Supporting Surfaces & Textures
- **Elevated Canvas (`#121214`)**: Surface layer for interactive cards, dialog overlays, and bento modules.
- **Charcoal Border Glass (`#26262B`)**: Translucent boundary stroke for structure and separation.
- **Parchment White (`#F7F4EA`)**: Primary typographic color, offering warmth and contrast against pure dark tones.
- **Muted Cream (`#DCD7CD`)**: Secondary typography, captions, metadata, and inactive icons.
- **Amber Glow (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`)**: Radial underglows and focal highlights behind primary imagery or buttons.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a dialogue between old-world luxury and contemporary precision:

- **Display & Headings (`Bodoni Moda`)**: An authoritative, high-contrast serif with razor serifs that project tailoring, heritage, and distinction. Titles, tier names, and signature service headings use this face.
- **Body Text (`Manrope`)**: A geometric, balanced modernist sans-serif offering neutral legibility, calm flow, and comfortable scanning for treatment descriptions and salon policies.
- **Labels, Numbers & Timers (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, futuristic grotesque typography reserved for pricing, time slots, service durations, badges, and uppercase category ribbons.

Always render labels and metadata tags in uppercase with tracking between `0.08em` and `0.14em`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an asymmetric **Luxury Bento Grid** system configured within a 12-column structure on desktop, a 6-column structure on tablet, and a 2-column or single-stack structure on mobile.

### Layout Principles
- **Atmospheric Margins**: Outer canvas bounds breathe deeply with generous side margins (`3rem` desktop) to maintain an intimate, salon-like experience.
- **Bento Modules**: Distinct grooming experiences (Royal Shave, Beard Sculpting, VIP Lounge, Master Specialists) are structured in varied module aspect ratios (e.g., spans of 4, 8, or 12 columns).
- **Rhythmic Vertical Stacking**: Sections are divided by fine horizontal hairline dividers (`#26262B`) accented with centered gold diamonds or emblems.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - *Desktop (1200px+)*: 12-column grid, 24px gutters, max-width 1360px container.
  - *Tablet (768px - 1199px)*: 6-column grid, 16px gutters, full horizontal stretch with 32px safe margins.
  - *Mobile (< 768px)*: Single vertical flow; bento grids reflow into swipeable horizontal rails or stacked cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; it relies on **Tonal Density, Glassmorphism, and Warm Gilded Backlighting**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0B0C`)**: The grounding level, deep and non-reflective.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Backgrounds)**: `#121214` rendered at 85% opacity with an underlying `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `#26262B`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Selected State & Dialog Overlays)**: `#19191D` with 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` and an ambient inner highlight glow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.2)`).
- **Gilded Luminescence**: Floating actions and featured VIP packages utilize an ambient amber/gold shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18), 0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy prioritizes architectural severity and masculine tailoring over playful pill forms. 

- Corners are subtly softened using `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` or `4px`), reflecting the beveled edge of heavy glass mirrors and straight razor scales.
- Larger interactive panels and bento enclosures employ `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` or `8px`).
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for barber avatar portraits, icon anchors, and circular calendar date pickers.
- Accent motifs include 45-degree angled chamfers or diamond bullet points.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Luxury Action (Book Appointment / Reserve)**:
  - Background: Solid brushed metallic gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E5C158 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #997A15 100%)`).
  - Text: `#0B0B0C`, uppercase `label-lg`, font weight 600.
  - Border: 1px solid `#F7F4EA` at 30% opacity.
  - Hover: Ambient gold underglow with slight brightness increase.
- **Secondary Action (View Service Details)**:
  - Background: `rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.8)` with backdrop blur.
  - Text: `#F7F4EA`.
  - Border: 1px solid `#26262B`.
  - Hover: Border color transitions to `#D4AF37`.

### Bento Cards & Service Tiles
- Background in `rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.85)` with `1px solid #26262B`.
- Top-right corner of each card hosts service duration or price in `Space Grotesk` (`#E5C158`).
- Hover state smoothly elevates the border to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)` with an inner amber rim glow.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact capsules (`0.25rem` radius) with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` fill and `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- Text styled in `label-sm` with gold tint (`#E5C158`). Used for "Master Barber", "Signature Ritual", and "Available Today".

### Form Controls & Inputs
- Background: `#0B0B0C`.
- Border: `1px solid #26262B`, transitioning to `1px solid #D4AF37` upon focus.
- Placeholder text in `#DCD7CD` with 40% opacity.
- Radio buttons and checkboxes are square with `2px` corner rounding, filled with solid `#D4AF37` and marked with obsidian `#0B0B0C` checkmarks.

### Bespoke Barbershop Additions
- **Barber Availability Switch**: Interactive schedule selector displaying current queue time, stylist station number, and active booking slots in monospace-styled `Space Grotesk`.
- **Treatment Ledger (List View)**: Minimalist tabular row with hair-fine dividers, left-aligned serif service name, dashed leader line, and bold right-aligned gold pricing.
